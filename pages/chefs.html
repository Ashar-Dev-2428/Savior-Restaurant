Executive Chef
Founder & Visionary

Marco Rossi

With over 25 years of culinary excellence, Chef Marco Rossi has shaped Savoir into one of the world's most celebrated dining destinations. Trained in the kitchens of Italy and France, he brings a unique perspective that bridges traditional European techniques with modern innovation.

Under his leadership, Savoir has earned two Michelin stars and a place among the World's 50 Best Restaurants. His philosophy is simple: respect the ingredient, master the technique, and create with passion.
