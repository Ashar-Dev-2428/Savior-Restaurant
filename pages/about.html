A Legacy of Culinary Excellence

Savoir was born from a simple yet profound belief: that dining should be an extraordinary experience that engages all the senses. Founded in 2009 by renowned chef Marco Rossi, our restaurant has grown from a humble bistro to one of the most celebrated fine dining destinations in the world.

Our journey has been defined by an unwavering commitment to excellence. Every ingredient is sourced with care, every dish is crafted with precision, and every guest is treated with the utmost respect. This dedication has earned us numerous accolades, including two Michelin stars and recognition from the World's 50 Best Restaurants.

Marco Rossi

Founder & Executive Chef

Our Mission

To create unforgettable dining experiences that celebrate the art of gastronomy. We strive to push the boundaries of culinary innovation while honoring traditional techniques and the finest ingredients nature has to offer.

  • Excellence in every detail
  • Sustainable sourcing practices
  • Innovation with respect for tradition

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as the pinnacle of fine dining, where every visit becomes a cherished memory. We envision a world where culinary artistry brings people together and creates moments of pure joy.

  • Global culinary leadership
  • Nurturing the next generation of chefs
  • Setting new standards in hospitality
