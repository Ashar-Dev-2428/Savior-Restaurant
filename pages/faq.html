Reservations

You can make a reservation through our website by filling out the reservation form, calling us directly at +1 (212) 555-7890, or sending an email to reservations@savoir.com. Online reservations receive instant confirmation.

We recommend booking at least 1-2 weeks in advance for regular dining and 1-2 months ahead for weekends and special occasions. For private dining events, we suggest booking 2-3 months in advance.

We require at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a fee of $50 per person. For private events and large parties (8+), 72 hours' notice is required.

Yes, you can modify your reservation by calling us directly or replying to your confirmation email. Changes are subject to availability and must be made at least 24 hours in advance.

Dining Experience

We recommend smart casual or formal attire. Gentlemen are encouraged to wear collared shirts and dress shoes. Please avoid athletic wear, shorts, flip-flops, or overly casual clothing to maintain our elegant atmosphere.

A typical dining experience lasts 2-3 hours for our à la carte menu and 3-4 hours for the tasting menu. We recommend allowing ample time to fully enjoy the experience without feeling rushed.

Yes, our sommelier offers curated wine pairings for both our à la carte and tasting menus. We also have an extensive wine list featuring over 500 labels from around the world. Non-alcoholic pairings are also available.

While we don't have a dedicated children's menu, we can accommodate younger guests with simplified preparations of our dishes. We recommend our restaurant for children 8 and older for the best experience.

Dietary Requirements

Absolutely. We accommodate vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and most other dietary requirements. Please inform us of any restrictions when making your reservation so our chefs can prepare accordingly.

Yes, we take allergies very seriously. Our kitchen is trained to handle allergies including nuts, shellfish, dairy, and gluten. Please inform us of any severe allergies when booking, and we'll ensure your safety.

We can accommodate halal and kosher-style preparations with advance notice. Please contact us directly at least 48 hours before your reservation to discuss specific requirements.

Private Events

Yes, we offer private dining rooms for events ranging from intimate dinners to corporate functions. Our events team can customize menus, décor, and service to your specific needs.

Our private dining rooms accommodate 8 to 50 guests. For larger events, we offer full restaurant buyouts accommodating up to 120 guests. Contact our events team for detailed information.

Yes, our executive chef will work with you to create a customized menu that reflects your preferences and event theme. We offer tastings prior to your event to ensure everything is perfect.

General

Yes, gift cards are available in denominations from $100 to $1,000. They can be purchased at the restaurant or ordered online for delivery. Gift cards never expire and can be used for dining and retail purchases.

We offer valet parking for $25. There are also several public parking garages within a two-block radius. We recommend arriving 15 minutes early to allow time for parking.

Yes, our restaurant is fully wheelchair accessible with ramps, elevators, and accessible restrooms. Please let us know if you need any special accommodations when making your reservation.

We allow outside wine with a corkage fee of $50 per bottle (limit 2 bottles per table). The wine must not be on our current wine list. Please inform us when making your reservation.
